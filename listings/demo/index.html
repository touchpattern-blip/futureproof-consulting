The Property

A 1923 Spanish bungalow on a 6,500 square foot R2 lot, three blocks from the Picfair Village strip.

Original oak floors run through all three bedrooms and the barrel-ceiling living room. The kitchen was rebuilt in 2021: quartz counters, a 36-inch range, and a pocket door to the laundry porch. Both baths keep their 1920s tile; the primary adds a walk-in shower and double vanity. Central heat and air, copper plumbing, and a 200-amp panel with an EV circuit in the driveway.

The lot is the story. R2 zoning, alley access at the rear, and a detached two-car garage already framed for conversion. Picfair Village sits between Midtown and Culver City: ten minutes to the studio lots on surface streets, four blocks to the 10, and a short walk to the coffee and bakeries on Pico.
