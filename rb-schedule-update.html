RB Schedule: keep the whole year + full details

Two small changes to the calendar sync you already have. About 10 minutes. Nothing new to install.

What this fixes: today the calendar only keeps last week forward, and the published feed hides the details. After this, the calendar keeps every job of the year with its order number, your hours, and notes. That covers Concur order numbers, survey-tool job info, and hours checks.

Part 1: Update the script

  1. Open the schedule workbook (SharePoint RBW Installations) in Excel for the web.
  2. Click the Automate tab, then All Scripts, then RB Schedule Sync, then Edit.
  3. Click inside the code and press Ctrl+A, then Delete.
  4. In a new tab open futureproofav.com/rb-schedule.txt, press Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C.
  5. Back in the script editor, Ctrl+V, then Save script.
  6. Click Run once. It should finish with no red error. (The only change: it now keeps rows from Jan 1 instead of from last week.)

Part 2: Let the published calendar show details

  1. Go to outlook.office.com, then the gear (Settings), then Calendar, then Shared calendars.
  2. Under Publish a calendar, choose RB Schedule.
  3. Set the permission to Can view all details, then click Publish (or change it if it already shows a lower level like "Can view when I'm busy" or "titles and locations").
  4. Look at the ICS link it shows.
    If the ICS link changed: copy it and paste it into drop.hyperchrist.com (PIN 47732) with the label rb-schedule-link. Meridian will re-subscribe the Mac and your iPhone follows via iCloud. If the link is the same as before, nothing else to do.

Part 3: Run it once and check

  1. Go to make.powerautomate.com, then My flows, then RB Schedule to Calendar, then Run, then Run flow. Wait for the green check (a minute or two; it now creates more events).
  2. Open the RB Schedule calendar in Outlook: jobs back to January should be there, and opening one should show Order:, Gio hrs:, and Notes:.
Done. From then on it refreshes daily on its own. It still only carries your own assigned jobs (rows with hours in your column), the same data already on your calendar.
