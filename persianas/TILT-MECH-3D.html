Hidden Tilt Strip · working model

center stile -- SECTIONED at the groove plane: solid behind the strip, ghosted in front. The strip rides in the JOINT between the stile and the slat ends
slat stubs -- pivot on the PIVOT PIN (1/4", on the slat centerline)
DRIVE PIN (3/16") at a 3/8" offset from the pivot = the crank arm
aluminum strip (1/8" x 3/4", slotted) -- captive in a groove OFFSET FORWARD of the pivot line, so the bar CLEARS the pivot pins; slides UP/DOWN only

Working range ±60° -- the slats SEAL by overlap at ~56°, so the pins never over-travel. Strip moves ~5/8" across that range. The open-edge notches absorb the drive pin's sideways swing; in the DRIVE direction the slot is a CLOSE FIT on the pin -- that contact is what moves the strip. Same crank arm on every slat = every slat length rotates identically.
